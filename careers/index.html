Build One World with us.

We are a small team building a place where small businesses get found, trusted and paid. Roles are remote.

Roles we hire for

  • Back-end software engineer: scalable APIs, services and cloud infrastructure.
  • UI/UX designer: the One World experience on web and mobile.
  • QA engineer: test plans, regression and automation across every flow.
  • Customer support lead: the voice of One World for our earliest members.
  • Marketing manager: growth across digital channels and partnerships.
  • Legal and compliance advisor: platform, payments and gig-economy rules.

How to apply

Email support@oneworldlabs.ai with the role in the subject, a short note and a link to your work. Equity may be available for founding-team roles.
