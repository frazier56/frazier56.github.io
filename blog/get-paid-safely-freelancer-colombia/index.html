How to get paid safely as a freelancer in Colombia

Short answer

To get paid safely as a freelancer in Colombia, agree the scope and price in writing before you start, use a payment method that protects both sides, and keep a record of what you delivered. Most unpaid-work stories start with a verbal deal.

Agree it in writing

Write down what you will deliver, the price, the deadline and how many revisions are included. A short message that both sides confirm is better than nothing. Contracts can be simple, but they should exist.

Protect the payment

Ask for a deposit or for payment to be held before you start, and release the work when you are paid. A protected payment means the client knows the money is real and you know it is waiting for you.

Keep a record

Save the agreement, the files you delivered and the messages where the client approved them. If there is ever a disagreement, a clear record settles it fast.

How OneJob helps

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a contract for a small job?

Yes, a short written agreement is enough, as long as both sides confirm it.

When should I deliver the files?

Once the payment is protected or paid, as agreed in writing.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. For contracts and taxes in Colombia, ask a local professional.
