How to price your photography in Medellín (2026)

Know the market, price the hours you do not see, and put the package in writing.

Start from what the market charges

Short event coverage in Medellín starts at about 350,000 pesos for 1 to 1.5 hours (Colombia Move, 2026), and wedding photography alone is commonly quoted at 3 to 10 million pesos (CuantoMeCuesta, April 2026). Where you sit in that range depends on your portfolio, your gear and how fast you deliver.

Price the hours nobody sees

A two-hour shoot is rarely two hours of work. Add travel, setup, culling, editing and delivery. A simple rule: count every hour the job takes you, then divide your price by that number. If the result is less than you want to earn per hour, the price is too low.

Sell packages, not hours

  • Essential: a set number of hours and edited photos.
  • Standard: more hours, more photos, a faster delivery date.
  • Complete: a second shooter, prints or an album, and a short video if you offer one.

Three clear options make it easy for a client to say yes to the middle one.

Put it in writing

Write the hours, the number of edited photos, the delivery date, usage rights (personal or advertising) and the cancellation terms.
