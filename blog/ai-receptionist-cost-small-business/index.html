What does an AI receptionist cost, and is it worth it for a small business?

Short answer

AI receptionist pricing usually combines a monthly fee with a number of included call minutes, and the right plan depends on how many calls you get. It is worth it when a missed call costs you a customer, a booking or a sale.

How they are priced

Most services charge a monthly fee that includes a set number of call minutes, with an extra rate for minutes above that. Some also charge a one-time setup fee. Prices vary a lot between providers, so compare the same things: monthly fee, included minutes, price per extra minute, setup fee and contract length.

Is it worth it?

Count the calls you miss in a normal week and multiply by what one new customer is worth to you. If a missed call from a buyer, a patient or a client costs you more than the monthly fee, an AI receptionist usually pays for itself. A business that rarely gets calls may not need one.

What to check before you buy

Can it answer in Spanish and English? Does it book appointments and send you the details by text or email? Can it hand a call to a person when needed? What happens to the call recordings and customer data?
