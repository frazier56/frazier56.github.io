How much does a videographer cost in Medellín? (2026)

Wedding and event prices, what drives the cost of video, and the five lines every video quote should have.

Short answer

For a wedding, photography and documentary video together are commonly quoted at 6 to 25 million pesos in Colombia (CuantoMeCuesta, April 2026). For smaller jobs we found no published Medellín video price list we trust; for comparison, short event photography starts at about 350,000 pesos for 1 to 1.5 hours (Colombia Move, 2026).

Why video costs more than it looks

Filming is the visible part. Editing, colour, sound and music licensing usually take longer than the shoot, and each round of changes adds time. A second camera, an audio operator or a drone (where it is allowed) adds people and gear.

The five lines every video quote should have

  1. Hours of filming, and the date and place.
  2. The finished videos: how many, how long, in what format (horizontal, vertical for Reels).
  3. Rounds of changes included.
  4. Delivery date.
  5. Who owns the footage, whether you get the raw files, and whether the videographer may use it in a portfolio.

  4. Leave a review. After the job, each of you reviews the other, so the next person can decide with more to go on.
