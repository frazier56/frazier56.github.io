Medellín airport (JMC) to El Poblado: price, time and options

Short answer

The official basic-taxi fare for the José María Córdova airport run is 118,000 pesos, and private transfer companies start at about 52 US dollars. The airport is outside the city, so the ride to El Poblado usually takes around an hour or more depending on traffic.

Your main options

Official taxi: the city sets a published fare for the airport run, 118,000 pesos for a basic taxi (Alcaldía de Medellín, fares from 1 March 2025). Private transfer: booking companies start at about 52 US dollars for a private car (Transfeero). Pre-booked driver: you agree the price and the pick-up point before you land.

Tips for arrival

Agree the price before you get in. Share your flight number so the driver can track delays. Keep the driver details in your phone and confirm the plate before you ride. Have a little cash in pesos for tolls if your driver asks.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the airport from El Poblado?

José María Córdova airport is in Rionegro, outside Medellín. Expect roughly an hour or more depending on traffic.

Should I book before I land?

Booking ahead lets you agree the price and meet your driver at an agreed place.

Are prices fixed?

The taxi fare is official. Private transfer prices vary by provider, so confirm before you ride.
