Renting an apartment in Medellín: what to check before you pay

Furnished or unfurnished, a month or a year: the questions that save you money and trouble.

Furnished or unfurnished

Furnished apartments (amoblados) suit stays of weeks or months and cost more per month. Unfurnished apartments are usually rented for a year or longer under Colombia's urban-lease law (Ley 820 de 2003), and landlords often ask for a co-signer (codeudor) or a rental insurance policy.

Before you pay anything

  • See the apartment, in person or on a live video call.
  • Confirm who the owner or authorised agent is.
  • Read the contract: monthly price, what it includes (administración, utilities, internet), length, and how either side can end it.
  • Photograph every room and anything damaged on the first day.
