Cleaning between guests: a guide for short-stay hosts in Medellín

Turnovers decide your reviews. Here is what cleaning costs and how to make it reliable.

What a day costs

Most cleaners in Medellín quote 60,000 to 100,000 pesos for a 7 to 8 hour day (Colombia Move, 2026). For a direct hire, the 2026 legal minimum for a full day is about 73,393 to 76,394 pesos, before social security (Noticias RCN). See the full cleaner price guide.

A turnover checklist

  • Linens and towels changed; a spare set in the closet.
  • Kitchen, bathroom and floors; trash out.
  • Restock: toilet paper, soap, coffee, water.
  • Photos of each room when done, sent to you.
  • Anything broken or missing, reported the same day.

Keep the arrangement clear

Agree a price per turnover or per day, who supplies products, and how keys and building access work. If you hire directly, remember the social-security rules. List your apartment on OneHome and agree the cleaning on OneJob, with the same account.
