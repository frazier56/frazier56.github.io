Event promoters in Medellín: filling rooms and proving it

A venue hires the promoter who can show the numbers. Here is how to keep them.

Proof beats promises

Every promoter says they can fill a room. The ones who get the next Saturday can show it: tickets sold, people checked in at the door, and what the night looked like.

Sell tickets and check people in

On OneEvent you publish the event, sell tickets and check guests in with a QR code at the door, so the count of who actually came is recorded, not estimated.

Put the deal with the venue in writing

  • Your share: per ticket, per head or a flat fee.
  • Guest list rules and cut-off times.
  • Who pays the DJ, photographer and door staff.
  • When you are paid.

Build the team once

A good night needs a DJ, a photographer and sometimes drivers for guests. Hiring them through OneJob keeps each agreement and payment in one place.
