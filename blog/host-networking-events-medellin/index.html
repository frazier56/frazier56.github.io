Hosting networking events in Medellín people come back to

Medellín's founder, expat and freelancer communities are full of meetups. The ones that last do a few things right.

Give people a reason to talk

Pick a theme narrow enough that strangers have something in common: founders hiring in Colombia, freelancers working with US clients, creators in Laureles. Introduce people to each other in the first twenty minutes; that is what guests remember.

Make arriving easy

Publish the event on OneEvent with the place, time and price in pesos or dollars, sell tickets or free spots, and check guests in with a QR code. You know who came, and next time you can invite them first.

Follow up within a day

Send a short thank-you with the date of the next event. Hosts who keep a steady date build a room that fills itself.

Hire help when it grows

A photographer for the recap, a chef or caterer for food, a DJ for the second half. Each one can be agreed and paid through OneJob.
