Planning a bachelor or bachelorette party in Medellín

A driver, a dinner, a DJ and photos: the four hires that make the weekend, and how to keep them straight.

The four hires groups make

  • Transport: a driver for the group, from airport pickup to a day in Guatapé. See private drivers.
  • A dinner at the apartment: private chefs average 33 to 47 US dollars per person for larger groups (Take a Chef). See private chefs.
  • Music: 45 to 120 US dollars an hour for a party DJ (Cue Up). See DJs.
  • Photos: short coverage from about 350,000 pesos (Colombia Move). See photographers.

Split it fairly

Decide early who pays what. One person can pay each vendor and the group pays them back, or each person pays their share directly; OnePay lets anyone send a payment by link or QR code.

Book with a written agreement

Every vendor gets the date, the hours, the place and the price in writing, plus what happens if plans change. With a group, plans change.

On OneJob, the agreement, the payment status and the reviews for each job live in one place. Eligible card payments are held until both sides mark the job complete; Nequi and PSE payments go through OnePay, in the same app; cash or a direct transfer is up to you and is not held by OneJob.
