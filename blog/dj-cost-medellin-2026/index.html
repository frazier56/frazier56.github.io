How much does a DJ cost in Medellín? (2026)

Hourly rates, party and wedding totals, and the questions that decide the final price, with the sources for every number.

Short answer

On booking sites, DJs in Medellín typically charge 45 to 120 US dollars an hour, about 80 on average, so a four-hour party lands around 180 to 480 US dollars (Cue Up). A five-hour wedding DJ is commonly quoted at 1.5 to 5 million pesos (CuantoMeCuesta, April 2026).

Why two quotes for the same night can differ by double

The biggest swing is equipment. A DJ who brings speakers sized for the room, a mixer, microphones for speeches and lighting is selling a production, not only a playlist. The second is hours: most DJs quote per set, and the last hour of a wedding is often the one that runs over. The third is the date. December, long weekends (puentes) and Saturdays fill first.

What to put in the DJ contract

  • Arrival time, setup time and the exact playing hours.
  • Who supplies speakers, mixer, microphones and lights, and the venue's power and space.
  • The price of each extra hour.
  • Music style, must-play and do-not-play lists, and any announcements.
  • Cancellation: what happens if either side cancels, and by when.
  • Travel costs for venues outside the metro area.
