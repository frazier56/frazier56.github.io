Jobs in Medellín for English speakers: where to look and what they pay

By Lee Frazier, founder of One World Labs · Published and last updated 27 November 2026

Short answer

English speakers in Medellín mostly find work in three places: bilingual customer-service (call-center) roles, English teaching, and freelancing for clients here or abroad. Published pay guides put bilingual call-center base pay at roughly COP 2.0–3.1 million a month and teaching at roughly COP 2.5–5 million, but none of those figures is an official Medellín statistic, so treat them as ranges to check.

Which jobs do English speakers actually get in Medellín?

Three groups come up again and again. First, bilingual customer-service roles at call-center and outsourcing companies, which sell English-language support to companies abroad. Second, English teaching at language institutes and private bilingual schools. Third, freelance work: writing, design, video, translation, tutoring or consulting, paid by clients in Colombia or abroad.

Corporate jobs at Colombian companies usually also expect Spanish. One guide says intermediate Spanish (B1 or better) is typical for local corporate roles; treat that as one source’s view, not a rule.

What do bilingual call-center jobs pay?

A June 2026 guide that checked live job postings reports base pay between COP 2.0 and 3.1 million a month and says most listings ask for B2 English. Another guide from the same publisher gives 2.5 to 4 million for bilingual call-center or teaching roles. The two ranges overlap but do not match, and neither names its postings.

For comparison, the 2026 legal minimum wage is COP 1,750,905 a month plus a COP 249,095 transport allowance for eligible employees. Always ask whether a quoted figure is base pay or includes bonuses.

What does teaching English pay?

The same publisher’s teaching guide (updated 15 June 2026) lists COP 2.5–3.5 million a month at language institutes, often for 20–30 hours a week, and COP 3–5 million at private bilingual schools. It says Bogotá tends to pay slightly more than Medellín but gives no numbers. Private tutoring is quoted per hour (roughly COP 70,000–150,000), with no source named.

Can I work in Colombia on a tourist stay?

The official Cancillería visa page says the visitor “Turismo” visa does not allow work in Colombian territory, and the same for the “Negocios” visa. Entry rules and visa categories differ by nationality and change, and this post is not legal advice. Check the official page and ask an immigration professional before you accept any job: https://cancilleria.gov.co/atencion-y-servicio-al-ciudadano/tramites-y-servicios/visa/tipos-de-visa

Where should I look for openings?

General job boards such as Computrabajo and Indeed Colombia list bilingual-agent roles in Medellín and nearby Sabaneta. Large outsourcing companies, for example Teleperformance, publish “Customer Expert” roles for Medellín on their own career sites (seen in search results on 27 November 2026; openings change daily). For freelance work, a profile with reviews and a credibility score helps clients choose you.

How do I get paid safely if I freelance?

Agree scope and price in writing before you start, use a protected payment, and keep a record of what you delivered. Our guide on getting paid safely as a freelancer in Colombia walks through it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Spanish to work in Medellín?

Not for every role. Bilingual customer-service roles for foreign clients mostly ask for English, with B2 level named in most listings checked by one guide in June 2026. Local Colombian employers usually expect Spanish as well.

What is the minimum wage in Colombia in 2026?

COP 1,750,905 a month, plus a COP 249,095 transport allowance for eligible employees, per La República (20 February 2026). The decree is described as transitional while a Consejo de Estado case is pending.

How much does a bilingual call-center job pay in Medellín?

One June 2026 guide reports base pay of COP 2.0–3.1 million a month across Colombia from postings it checked; it gives no Medellín-only figure. Check each posting.

How much can I earn teaching English?

One guide (updated 15 June 2026) lists COP 2.5–3.5 million a month at language institutes and COP 3–5 million at private bilingual schools. It names no data source.

Can I work on a tourist visa?

The official Cancillería page says the V Turismo visa does not permit work in Colombia. Check the official page and an immigration professional for your own case.

What English level do employers ask for?

In the June 2026 guide, most bilingual call-center postings named B2. Requirements vary by employer.

Can I freelance for clients abroad from Medellín?

Many people do. Tax and immigration treatment depends on your situation, so ask a Colombian professional. This post is not legal or tax advice.

Where do I find jobs in Medellín for English speakers?

Job boards like Computrabajo and Indeed Colombia, the career sites of outsourcing companies, and freelance platforms such as OneJob.

Are the pay figures in this post official?

No. They come from published guides and a news article, each cited with its date. None is an official Medellín statistic, and pay changes.
