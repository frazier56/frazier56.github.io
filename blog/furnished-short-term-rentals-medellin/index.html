Furnished, monthly and short-term apartments in Medellín: how to search

Neighbourhoods, the questions to ask, and what to check before you send money.

Short answer

For stays of a few days to a few months, look for furnished apartments (amoblados) in El Poblado, Laureles, Envigado or Sabaneta, ask exactly what the price includes, see the apartment before you pay, and get the terms in writing. A one-year lease is a different contract from a short stay, so ask which one the host is offering.

Where people look

  • El Poblado: the best-known area for visitors and expats, with restaurants and nightlife close by.
  • Laureles: a flatter, more residential area that many people use for longer stays.
  • Envigado and Sabaneta: quieter areas to the south, popular with people who want more space.

Questions to ask the host

  • What does the price include: building fees (administración), water, electricity, gas, internet, cleaning?
  • What is the minimum stay, and what is the price by the day, week and month?
  • Is the internet fast enough for video calls? Ask for a speed test.
  • What is the deposit, and when and how is it returned?
  • Who is the owner or authorised agent?

Check before you pay

See the apartment in person or on a live video call, photograph the condition on day one, and never pay the full amount to someone you have not been able to identify. Our guides on spotting rental scams and what to check before you rent go through the details.
