5 ways to stand out when clients are choosing a pro

Clients decide in seconds. Give them what they look for first.

1. Say what you do in one line

"Wedding and event photographer in Medellín, English and Spanish" beats a paragraph. Put the city and the languages in it.

2. Show the work

Photos and short videos of real jobs: the room you played, the apartment you cleaned, the car you drive. Recent beats perfect.

3. Publish a starting price

"From 350,000 pesos" answers the first question before it is asked. Clients skip pros who make them guess.

4. Answer fast

The first pro to reply with a clear quote often gets the job. Turn on notifications and keep a short message ready.

5. Ask for the review every time

Reviews from real jobs are what the next client reads. Ask the same day, while the job is fresh. Coming soon, OneScore will bring a member's connected accounts, reviews and paid work together into one credibility score.
