Upwork and Fiverr alternative for Latin America: hire and get hired locally

Short answer

An Upwork or Fiverr alternative for Latin America should connect you with people in your own city, work in Spanish and English, and protect the payment until the job is done. OneJob is built around those three things.

Why look for an alternative

Global freelance sites are crowded, and most of the competition is far from the job. If you need a videographer, a cleaner or a driver in Medellín, or you are one, a local marketplace puts you in front of the people who can actually hire you.

What to compare

Look at where the people are, the languages the platform supports, how payment is protected and how you can show you are trustworthy. A credibility score and real reviews tell a client more than a long bio.

How OneJob works

A client posts the job or contacts a professional directly. Both sides agree on the terms in the app. Payment is protected until the work is marked done, and the finished job adds to the professional's OneScore and reviews.

Frequently asked questions

Who is OneJob for?

Freelancers and service professionals such as videographers, photographers, DJs, drivers, cleaners and designers, and the people who hire them.

Does it work in Spanish and English?

Yes.

Is the payment protected?

Payment is held until both sides mark the job complete.
