How much does a cleaner by the day cost in Medellín? (2026)

Market rates, the legal floor for 2026 and what the law asks of you when you hire someone directly.

Short answer

Most quotes in Medellín are 60,000 to 100,000 pesos for a 7 to 8 hour day, with El Poblado at the upper end (Colombia Move, 2026). If you hire the person directly, the 2026 legal minimum for a full day is about 73,393 to 76,394 pesos, before social security (Noticias RCN, July 2026).

Where the legal minimum comes from

The 2026 monthly minimum wage is 1,750,905 pesos, plus a transport allowance of 249,095 pesos (Portafolio, October 2026). A day rate for domestic work is worked out from that monthly figure, which is why the legal floor for a full day sits in the low 70,000s. Work after 7 p.m. carries a night surcharge.

What you owe when you hire directly

Hiring a cleaner yourself, even one day a week, makes you the employer. According to Noticias RCN, that means health and pension contributions by the weeks worked, occupational-risk insurance (ARL) from the first day, the family fund (caja de compensación), and prima and cesantías in proportion to the time worked. An agency that employs the cleaner handles this itself, which is part of why agencies charge more.

OneJob records what you agreed and the payment. It does not file social-security contributions for you.

Checklist before the first day

  • Hours, days and what is included: laundry, ironing, cooking, windows.
  • The day rate and how it is paid.
  • Who supplies cleaning products.
  • Keys and building access.
  • References you can call. One World does not run background checks.
