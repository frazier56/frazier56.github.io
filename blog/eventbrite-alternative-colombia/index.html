Eventbrite alternative in Colombia: how to sell tickets for your event

Short answer

If you host events in Colombia, the best Eventbrite alternative is a ticketing app made for local events: prices in pesos, QR check-in at the door and a place where nearby guests already look for things to do. OneEvent is built for that.

What to look for

Check four things before you pick a ticketing tool. First, can guests see the price in Colombian pesos? Second, can you check people in at the door with a QR code on a phone? Third, will local guests find your event inside the app, or do you have to bring every buyer yourself? Fourth, can you see exactly what you will be paid and when?

Why local matters

Many ticketing tools are built for the United States and Europe. For a local event, the checkout can feel foreign to guests, and few people browsing a global platform live nearby. A local listing in front of people who live close by matters as much as the checkout itself.

How it works on OneEvent

You create the event with a photo, date, place and ticket types. Guests find it in the feed, buy a ticket and get a QR code. At the door you scan the code to check them in. Your profile carries your OneScore and reviews from past events, so new guests can see you are real.

Frequently asked questions

Can I host a free event?

Yes. You can list a free event so guests can reserve a spot and check in with a QR code.

Do I need a website?

No. Your event page lives inside the app and can be shared as a link.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. The app and event pages work in English and Spanish.
