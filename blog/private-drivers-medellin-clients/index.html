Private drivers in Medellín: how to build a steady client base

Airport runs, weddings, day trips to Guatapé: the drivers who get booked again are the ones clients can trust.

What clients are really paying for

  • Punctuality: there when you said, with a message before you arrive.
  • Discretion: professional conduct and privacy.
  • A clean vehicle suited to the occasion.
  • Clear prices: one total that names hours, waiting, tolls and fuel.

Know the reference prices

Clients compare. The official basic-taxi fare to José María Córdova airport is 118,000 pesos (Alcaldía de Medellín), private transfer companies start at about 52 US dollars (Transfeero), and a private car to Guatapé starts at about 29 US dollars per person (Daytrip). Price with those numbers in mind and explain what your service adds.

Stay on the right side of the rules

Driving a client's own car (conductor elegido) is different from carrying passengers in your car for a fare, which Colombian rules reserve for licensed companies and vehicles (Colombia Move summary of Ley 336 de 1996). Offer the service you are allowed to offer, and say clearly which one it is.

Turn one ride into a relationship

  1. Ask happy clients for a review the same day.
  2. Leave your profile link with wedding planners, hotels and event hosts, who book again and again.
  3. Keep every booking in writing, so there is never a question about the price.
