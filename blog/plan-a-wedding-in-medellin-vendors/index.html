Planning a wedding in Medellín: hiring every vendor in one place

Photographer, video, DJ, makeup, food and transport: what each typically costs and what to agree in writing.

The vendors, and what they typically cost

  • Photography and video: 6 to 25 million pesos together; photography alone 3 to 10 million (CuantoMeCuesta, April 2026). See photographers and videographers.
  • DJ: 1.5 to 5 million pesos for five hours (CuantoMeCuesta). See DJs in Medellín.
  • Makeup: bridal makeup with a trial 300,000 to 900,000 pesos (Gestabiz, 2026). See makeup artists.
  • Transport: quoted per trip or per block of hours. See private drivers.

One contract per vendor

Every vendor gets the same five lines in writing: date and hours, what is included, the price and how it is paid, the delivery date (for photo and video), and what happens if either side cancels.

Keep it in one place

The hardest part of a wedding is not finding vendors; it is keeping ten conversations, ten prices and ten payment dates straight.

On OneJob, the agreement, the payment status and the reviews for each job live in one place. Eligible card payments are held until both sides mark the job complete; Nequi and PSE payments go through OnePay, in the same app; cash or a direct transfer is up to you and is not held by OneJob.
