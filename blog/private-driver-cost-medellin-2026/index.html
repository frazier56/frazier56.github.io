How much does a private driver cost in Medellín? (2026)

Reference prices for the trips people book most, how hourly quotes work, and what to check before you ride.

Short answer

There is no official hourly rate for a private driver (Colombia Move). For reference: the official basic-taxi fare to José María Córdova airport is 118,000 pesos (Alcaldía de Medellín), private transfer companies start at about 52 US dollars (Transfeero), and a private car to Guatapé starts at about 29 US dollars per person (Daytrip).

How hourly quotes work

Hourly hire is usually sold as a block of hours with a kilometre allowance; Uber's Por Tiempo, for example, offers packages from 1 hour and 15 km up to 4 hours and 60 km. When you ask an independent driver for a quote, ask for one total that names the hours, the kilometres, waiting time, tolls, fuel and how the driver gets home.

Designated driver or passenger transport?

A driver who drives your car (conductor elegido) is a person you hire. A driver who brings the car and charges you for the trip is passenger transport, which Colombian rules reserve for licensed companies and vehicles, as Colombia Move summarises from Ley 336 de 1996. If the driver supplies the car, ask whether it is registered for that service and insured for passengers. This is general information, not legal advice.

Checklist before the trip

  • Pickup and drop-off points and times, and the waiting time included.
  • Tolls and fuel: included or extra.
  • The driver's licence and, if they bring a car, its registration and insurance.
  • Your own car insurance, if someone else will drive your car.

Questions people ask

How much is a private driver per hour in Medellín?

There is no official hourly rate. Ask for a total that names hours, kilometres, waiting, tolls and fuel.

How much is a ride to Medellín's airport?

The official basic-taxi fare to José María Córdova airport is 118,000 pesos (fares in force from 1 March 2025). Private transfer companies start at about 52 US dollars.

How much is a private car to Guatapé?

One booking site lists a private car from Medellín to Guatapé from about 29 US dollars per person, for a trip of about 1 hour 50 minutes each way.
