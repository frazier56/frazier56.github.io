Is renting an apartment online in Colombia safe? How to spot rental scams

Short answer

Renting online in Colombia can be safe if you verify the owner, visit the apartment before paying anything and sign a written contract. The common scams ask for money first and show you photos of a place that is not theirs.

Warning signs

The price is far below similar places nearby. The person says they are abroad and cannot show the place. You are asked to send money before you visit, or to pay through a method you do not recognise. The photos appear on other listings with a different address.

A safe checklist

See the apartment in person, or ask for a live video walk-through, before you pay. Ask for the owner or agent identification and check that it matches the name on the contract. In Colombia, a property record called certificado de tradición y libertad shows who legally owns a property, and anyone can request it. Get the agreement in writing and keep a copy. Pay only through a traceable method and keep every receipt.

Frequently asked questions

Should I pay a deposit before seeing the apartment?

No. Visit first, confirm who the owner is and read the contract before you pay anything.

How do I check who owns a property in Colombia?

Ask for the certificado de tradición y libertad, the public property record, and compare the owner name with the person you are dealing with.

Is this legal advice?

No. This is a general safety checklist. For a specific contract, ask a Colombian lawyer.
