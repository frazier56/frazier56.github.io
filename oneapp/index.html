Good questions
Everything you're wondering
Is it really a real app?
Yes — a real listing on the Apple App Store and Google Play that your customers search for, download, and get an icon for on their home screen. Not a bookmark, not a "save this page" trick. That's the whole point of the $499: getting through Apple's and Google's front doors is the hard part.
What does the $499 actually cover?
One time, everything from your approved design to a live download: opening both developer accounts with you, building the app, writing the store listing, producing the icon and screenshots, answering the privacy and age-rating questionnaires, submitting to both stores, and handling whatever the reviewers come back with. Apple's $99/year and Google's $25 one-time fees are separate and paid by you directly to them.
Why do the accounts have to be in my name?
Because it protects you. Apple and Google want the real business behind the app, and an app published under someone else's account can be taken away from you. Yours means the listing, the reviews, the download numbers and the app itself belong to your business permanently — you could stop working with us tomorrow and keep all of it. We walk you through the setup on a screen-share; it usually takes about twenty minutes.
How long until it's live?
Typically one to two weeks from the day you pay. Our part takes a few days; the rest is Apple's and Google's review queues, which we don't control. Google is usually faster. If a reviewer asks for a change, we make it and resubmit at no extra cost.
Do I need a website first?
No. They do different jobs — a website is how new customers find you, an app is how existing customers come back. If you don't have a site yet, our sister service OnePage builds you one free too, and the two work together.
What happens after it's live?
Your app keeps working. Apple and Google change their rules once or twice a year, and apps that aren't kept current eventually get pulled — so we offer optional care at $97/month to handle updates, resubmissions, content changes and monitoring. It's genuinely optional and you can start it later or cancel anytime.
What if I don't buy?
Nothing happens. Look at your design, sleep on it, come back. We'll email you a link so you don't lose it. There's no card and no call required to see it.
