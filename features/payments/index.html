Questions people ask

Is One World a bank?

No. Card, Nequi and PSE payments are processed by a payment provider. One World records each payment and shows its status.

When do I receive the money?

Payout times for card, Nequi and PSE depend on the provider and your bank. Cash and transfers are yours the moment they arrive.

Can I pay in dollars?

In OnePay you can set an amount in pesos or dollars. A dollar amount is converted to pesos at the TRM.
