Questions people ask

What does it cost?

One Fee line: the payment processor's cost plus One World's 1%. You choose who pays it.

Can I change a payment after I create it?

Yes. You can edit a payment until it is paid.

Does OnePay hold my money?

Card, Nequi and PSE payments are processed by a payment provider. OnePay is not a bank: it records each payment and shows its status until the money reaches you.

Questions people ask

Who is OnePay for?

Independent professionals who charge after a service, small shops entering a quick amount or picking items, and owners who want to see what their staff took today.

Can I take card payments on my phone?

Yes, by link or QR code: the customer pays by card on their own phone. Card tap on your phone comes after provider approval.

How can people pay?

With Nequi, PSE or a card.

Can I set the amount in dollars?

Yes. Choose pesos or dollars. A dollar amount is converted to pesos at the TRM, Colombia's official exchange rate.

When do I receive the money?

A payment shows as paid only when the money is confirmed. Cash and transfers are yours the moment they arrive; payout times for card, Nequi and PSE depend on the provider and your bank.
