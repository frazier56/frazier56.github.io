Phase 3 · Sell

Sell it your way — instant or curated.

Every event picks its own mode — from zero-friction to fully curated:

⚡ Free RSVP — instant ticket 🎟️ Paid — instant ticket 📝 Apply → auto-approve 🛡️ Apply → you review · card held

And that last mode is the part almost nobody else has: gated, application-based ticketing where the guest's card is authorized but not charged until you approve them.

🛡️

Hold the money, not the risk

Cards are held, charged only on approval. Decline someone and the hold is released — they were never charged, so there is nothing to charge back.

📝

Custom application forms

Ask exactly what you need — revenue band, niche, LinkedIn, anything — with a form builder built in.

🤖

VAIA scores every applicant

Describe your ideal guest once; every application gets a 0–10 fit score, red to green, right on the row.

✅

Approve, reject, undo

Full back-office: review applications one by one or in bulk, with templates for decline reasons — and an undo if you tap wrong.

🔔

Guests always know where they stand

Authorized, pending, approved, declined — guests are told in-app, by email and by text, every step.

💳

Wise & PayPal options

Prefer direct payment links alongside cards? Turn them on per event.
