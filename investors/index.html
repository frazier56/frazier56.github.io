Three scenarios, one honest read

Each scenario starts from a rentals-per-month path and runs a 36 month plan that spends a 1.5 million dollar raise (30,000 dollars a month in year 1, 42,000 in year 2, 52,000 in year 3). The raise size is a modeling assumption, not an offer.

What this says. On rental fees alone, only the strong case funds itself inside three years. The middle case needs about five more months than the plan covers, and the careful case needs either more capital or other revenue lines. We show that rather than hide it.

Method and limits

Rentals follow a compound growth path fitted to two points (month 12 and month 36). Net revenue is rentals times fee less card cost. Cash is the raise plus net revenue less spend. The model counts rental fees only, assumes no price change, and treats each scenario as a smooth curve, so it cannot show seasonality or a slow start. Adoption in a two sided marketplace is the main uncertainty, and nothing here is a forecast of results.
