One place where small businesses get found, get paid and get believed.

Scattered tools do not build credibility. One World puts advertising, payments and reviews in one account, and turns them into a standardized credibility score that customers can read at a glance.

Draft for review. Not an offer to sell securities.

The problem

A small business today runs on five or six disconnected tools: an ad account, a booking page, a payment app, a review site and a social feed. Customers can not tell who is real, and good businesses drown in noise and ad costs.

Noise

Reaching the right customers on social platforms means paid ads and clutter.

Scattered proof

Reviews, work and payment history live in different places, so no single signal builds trust.

Cross-border friction

In Colombia, rents, events and services are paid across banks, cards and cash with no shared record.

The product

One account, one profile and one score across the apps. Members identify as who they are (agent, creator, business), not by app.

OneHome

Rentals and sales with agent import (Wasi), host payouts and, for sales, a licensed fiduciary partner.

OneEvent

Events, tickets and reviews that feed a member's profile.

OneJob

Hire a person for a job, with a contract, payment and history.

OneScore

A standardized credibility score built from the reviews, work and payments a member connects. It is a profile-strength score, not a credit or background check.

OnePay

Pay and get paid through local Colombian rails and international cards.

OneAgent, OneRide, One Business

Agents' client work, booked private drivers and the business owner's home base.

How One World earns

A percentage on money that moves through the platform, never a flat fee per night. Free to list and join.

Risks we state plainly

Payments licensing

Third-party collection in Colombia depends on our payment partner's written approval. Pending.

Fiduciary guarantee

Commission on sales is only guaranteed once a fiduciary signs a commission agreement.

Adoption

Marketplaces need supply and demand together. The model shows cash shortfalls in two of three scenarios.

Regulation

Rentals, deposits, rides and scoring each have their own rules. Counsel reviews every money and trust claim.
