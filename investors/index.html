Three ways One World earns

One World does not hold customer funds. Each stream below is modeled separately, and all three feed one revenue line.

1 · Transactions

A fee on every payment

Rentals keep 8.99 percent. Other payments through One World add 1 percent today, and up to about 2 percent more in some cases. The processor's own fee is paid by the payer on top.

2 · Home sales

0.25 percent commission

On each home sold through OneHome. Small per home, large in volume, and backed by a fiduciary partner once the agreement is signed.

3 · Advertising

Free now, paid later

Advertising is free while the network grows. Businesses pay to promote once there is an audience. The model starts charging at the month you choose.

Where the money moves, and where it does not earn

Colombians pay in pesos through Wompi. Foreign cards and dollars go through Stripe. Both are processors: they charge their own fee, and One World adds its fee on top. That is how every payment is recorded and feeds the business dashboard and the credibility score.

Method and limits

Rentals follow a compound growth path fitted to two points (month 12 and month 36). Net revenue is rentals times fee less card cost, plus the One World fee on other payment volume, plus 0.25 percent of home sales, plus advertising from its start month. The processor fee on other payments is paid by the payer, so it is not a cost to One World. Cash is the raise plus net revenue less spend. The curve is smooth, so it cannot show seasonality or a slow start. Volume, home-sale and advertising levels are assumptions, not measured results; rentals are the only stream with a fitted history. Adoption in a two sided marketplace is the main uncertainty.

Risks we state plainly

Adoption

Marketplaces need supply and demand together. In the careful case the model runs out of cash.

Outside rails

Zelle, PayPal, Wise and direct Nequi earn nothing and leave no record. Revenue depends on members choosing to pay inside.

Payments licensing

Third-party collection in Colombia depends on our payment partner's written approval. Pending.

Fiduciary guarantee

Commission on sales is only guaranteed once a fiduciary signs a commission agreement.
