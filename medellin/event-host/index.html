Hire an event host or MC in Medellín

Weddings, company events, birthdays and launches: a good host keeps the night moving. Here is what to agree with an event host or MC in Medellín and how to book one through OneJob.

How pricing works

No public price range

We did not find a public price range for event hosts or MCs in Medellín that we trust, so we are not printing a number. Ask for quotes per event and per hour, and compare at least three. OneJob is new in Medellín and the first profiles are still being created. If you do this work, a free profile puts you in front of people searching for it.

What changes the price

  • Event type and length: a 3-hour launch and a 6-hour wedding are different jobs.
  • Languages: bilingual hosts (Spanish and English) are usually priced higher.
  • Preparation: a script, a run-of-show and a meeting with you before the event.
  • Extras: working with the DJ, games and activities, microphone and sound.
  • Date: December, long weekends and Saturdays book up first.

How hiring through OneJob works

  1. Find the person. Search pros in OneJob, or open a profile someone sent you. Profiles show what the person does, their photos and videos, and reviews from past jobs.
  2. Put it in writing. Start a contract with the date, hours, place, what is included and the price, in pesos or dollars. Both of you accept it in the app.
  3. Pay your way. Card payments made through OneJob on eligible contracts are held and released after you both mark the job complete. To pay a person in pesos with Nequi or PSE, use OnePay, part of the same app, by link or QR code. Cash or a direct transfer is up to you, but OneJob does not hold that money.
  4. Leave a review. After the job, each of you reviews the other, so the next person can decide with more to go on.

OneScore, a credibility score built from a member's connected accounts, reviews and paid work, is open now in the One World app.

Questions people ask

How do I hire an event host in Medellín?

Decide the event type, date, length and language, then ask for quotes from several hosts, watch a video of them working, and agree the run-of-show and the price in writing. You can search and contract through OneJob.

What does an MC do at a wedding or corporate event?

An MC welcomes guests, introduces speakers and moments such as the first dance or the toast, keeps the schedule, and coordinates with the DJ and the venue.
