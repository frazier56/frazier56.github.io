Typical ranges

WhatTypical rangeSource
One walk, Medellín (2022 figures, the latest we found)about 5,000 to 15,000 pesosWamiz — Dog walkers in Colombia: what they charge (2022)

Typical ranges from public listings and guides, checked on 12 November 2026. They are not One World prices: each pro sets their own, and you agree the price with them.

These are 2022 figures and prices have risen since; we did not find a newer published range for Medellín. Ask for the price per walk and per month, how long each walk lasts, and whether your dog walks alone or in a group.

What changes the price

  • Walk length (30, 45 or 60 minutes) and how many days a week.
  • Alone or in a small group.
  • Size and behaviour of the dog.
  • Keys and building access, if the walker collects the dog.

Before you hire

One World does not run background or criminal-record checks. Read the profile and reviews, ask for references, and meet the person before the first job.

How hiring through OneJob works

  1. Find the person. Search pros in OneJob, or open a profile someone sent you. Profiles show what the person does, their photos and videos, and reviews from past jobs.
  2. Put it in writing. Start a contract with the date, hours, place, what is included and the price, in pesos or dollars. Both of you accept it in the app.
  3. Pay your way. Card payments made through OneJob on eligible contracts are held and released after you both mark the job complete. To pay a person in pesos with Nequi or PSE, use OnePay, part of the same app, by link or QR code. Cash or a direct transfer is up to you, but OneJob does not hold that money.
  4. Leave a review. After the job, each of you reviews the other, so the next person can decide with more to go on.

Questions people ask

How much is a dog walker in Medellín?

The latest published figures we found (2022) put a walk at about 5,000 to 15,000 pesos in Medellín. Prices have risen since, so ask for a current quote per walk and per month.

What should I agree before the first walk?

Walk length, days and times, alone or in a group, what to do in an emergency, your vet's details and who holds the keys.

Does One World check walkers?

No. One World does not run background checks. Meet the walker first and read their reviews.
