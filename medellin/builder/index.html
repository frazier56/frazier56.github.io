What changes the price

  • Day rate or price per square metre: agree which, and what is measured.
  • Materials: who buys them and who is responsible for waste and delivery.
  • Experience and the size of the crew.
  • Access, floor level and demolition or cleanup.
  • Structural work, extensions or new builds: these usually need plans and a permit, and a professional such as an architect or engineer.

Before you hire

Before structural work, ask who obtains the building permit (licencia de construcción) and who is responsible for the plans. Ask who pays the workers' social security and insurance, agree the payment schedule in stages tied to finished work, and keep the final payment until the job is complete. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

One World does not run background or criminal-record checks. Read the profile and reviews, ask for references, and meet the person before the first job.

Questions people ask

How much does a builder cost per day in Medellín?

One estimating site (31 July 2026) lists about 95,000 to 160,000 pesos a day for a maestro de obra, 75,000 to 110,000 for an oficial and 55,000 to 75,000 for a helper, with Medellín at the higher end. Materials are extra and each builder sets their own price.

How much does tiling or plastering cost per square metre?

The same site lists interior plastering at about 18,000 to 35,000 pesos per square metre and ceramic floor tiling at about 28,000 to 52,000, labour only.

Do I need a permit to remodel in Medellín?

Structural changes, extensions and new builds usually need a building permit; small interior finishes usually do not. Ask the contractor and, for structural work, an architect or engineer who handles the permit.

How should I pay a contractor?

Pay in stages tied to finished work, keep the last payment for completion and put the scope, the schedule and the price in writing. OneJob contracts hold eligible card payments until you both mark the job complete.

Does One World check builders?

No. One World does not run background checks or check licences. Ask for references and visit a finished job.
