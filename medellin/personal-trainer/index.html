Typical ranges from public listings and guides, checked on 12 November 2026. They are not One World prices: each pro sets their own, and you agree the price with them.

What changes the price

  • Qualifications and specialities (rehabilitation, pregnancy, older adults, sport).
  • At your home or a gym, and travel time.
  • Packages: monthly plans usually cost less per session.
  • Nutrition plans, which some trainers price separately.

How hiring through OneJob works

  1. Find the person. Search pros in OneJob, or open a profile someone sent you. Profiles show what the person does, their photos and videos, and reviews from past jobs.
  2. Put it in writing. Start a contract with the date, hours, place, what is included and the price, in pesos or dollars. Both of you accept it in the app.
  3. Pay your way. Card payments made through OneJob on eligible contracts are held and released after you both mark the job complete. To pay a person in pesos with Nequi or PSE, use OnePay, part of the same app, by link or QR code. Cash or a direct transfer is up to you, but OneJob does not hold that money.
  4. Leave a review. After the job, each of you reviews the other, so the next person can decide with more to go on.

OneScore, a credibility score built from a member's connected accounts, reviews and paid work, is open now in the One World app.

Questions people ask

How much does a personal trainer cost in Medellín?

On one listing site, trainers in Medellín list about 25,000 to 120,000 pesos an hour, with an average of about 41,859 pesos (Superprof). Monthly packages usually lower the price per session.

Should I pay by session or by month?

Either works. For a package, write the number of sessions, how long they are valid and what happens with a cancelled session.

Is the trainer certified?

Ask. One World does not check qualifications; the profile shows what the trainer says about their training and reviews from past clients.
