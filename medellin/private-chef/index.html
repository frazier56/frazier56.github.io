What changes the price

  • The menu and ingredients (seafood and imported products cost more).
  • Number of guests: the price per person falls as the group grows.
  • Service: table service and cleaning the kitchen afterwards.
  • Drinks and tableware, which are often not included.

How hiring through OneJob works

  1. Find the person. Search pros in OneJob, or open a profile someone sent you. Profiles show what the person does, their photos and videos, and reviews from past jobs.
  2. Put it in writing. Start a contract with the date, hours, place, what is included and the price, in pesos or dollars. Both of you accept it in the app.
  3. Pay your way. Card payments made through OneJob on eligible contracts are held and released after you both mark the job complete. To pay a person in pesos with Nequi or PSE, use OnePay, part of the same app, by link or QR code. Cash or a direct transfer is up to you, but OneJob does not hold that money.
  4. Leave a review. After the job, each of you reviews the other, so the next person can decide with more to go on.

Questions people ask

How much is a private chef in Medellín?

On one booking site, private chefs in Medellín average about 62 US dollars per person for two guests, 47 for three to six, 41 for seven to twelve and 33 for thirteen or more. Each chef sets their own price.

What is included?

Usually the agreed menu, the ingredients, cooking at your home, serving and cleaning the kitchen. Drinks and tableware often are not. Write what is included in the contract.

Can the chef handle allergies or diets?

Tell the chef before you agree the menu and put the allergies in the contract.
