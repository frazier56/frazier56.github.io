What changes the price

  • Trip or hours: one-way trips are quoted per trip; time with you is quoted as a block of hours and kilometres.
  • Waiting time, tolls, fuel and the driver's way home.
  • Vehicle size: a van for a family or group costs more than a car.
  • Night and holiday hours.

Before you hire

Two different services share the name. A driver who drives YOUR car (conductor elegido) is hiring a person. A driver who brings the car and charges you for the trip is passenger transport, which Colombian rules reserve for licensed companies and vehicles (Colombia Move summary of Ley 336 de 1996). If the driver supplies the car, ask whether it is registered for that service and insured for passengers.

One World does not run background or criminal-record checks. Read the profile and reviews, ask for references, and meet the person before the first job.

How hiring through OneJob works

  1. Find the person. Search pros in OneJob, or open a profile someone sent you. Profiles show what the person does, their photos and videos, and reviews from past jobs.
  2. Put it in writing. Start a contract with the date, hours, place, what is included and the price, in pesos or dollars. Both of you accept it in the app.
  3. Pay your way. Card payments made through OneJob on eligible contracts are held and released after you both mark the job complete. To pay a person in pesos with Nequi or PSE, use OnePay, part of the same app, by link or QR code. Cash or a direct transfer is up to you, but OneJob does not hold that money.
  4. Leave a review. After the job, each of you reviews the other, so the next person can decide with more to go on.

Questions people ask

How much is a private driver in Medellín?

Prices are quoted per trip or per block of hours. For reference: the official basic-taxi fare to José María Córdova airport is 118,000 pesos, private transfer companies start at about 52 US dollars, and a private car to Guatapé starts at about 29 US dollars per person.

Can someone drive my own car?

Yes, that is a designated driver (conductor elegido). Agree the start and end points, the waiting time and how the driver gets home, and check your car insurance covers another driver.

Does One World check drivers?

One World does not run background or criminal-record checks for OneJob. Read the profile and reviews, and check the licence and, if they bring a car, its registration and insurance.
