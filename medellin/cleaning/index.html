What changes the price

  • Size of the home and what is included (laundry, ironing, cooking, windows).
  • Neighbourhood: quotes in El Poblado run higher than in Laureles or Envigado.
  • Agency or independent: agencies charge more and handle the paperwork.
  • Hours: past 7 p.m. a night surcharge applies to a direct hire.

Before you hire

If you hire a cleaner directly, even for one day a week, Colombian law treats you as the employer: health, pension, occupational-risk insurance (ARL, from the first day) and family-fund contributions, plus prima and cesantías in proportion to the days worked (Noticias RCN, July 2026). OneJob records the agreement and the payment; it does not file these contributions for you.

One World does not run background or criminal-record checks. Read the profile and reviews, ask for references, and meet the person before the first job.

Questions people ask

How much do I pay a cleaner for a day in Medellín?

Most quotes in Medellín are 60,000 to 100,000 pesos for a 7 to 8 hour day. For a direct hire in 2026, the legal minimum for a full day is about 73,393 to 76,394 pesos, before social-security contributions.

Do I have to pay social security for a cleaner by the day?

Yes, if you hire the person directly. Health, pension, ARL from the first day and the family fund apply, in proportion to the days worked. An agency that employs the cleaner handles this itself.

Does One World check cleaners' backgrounds?

No. One World does not run background or criminal-record checks. Read the person's profile and reviews, ask for references, and meet before giving keys.
