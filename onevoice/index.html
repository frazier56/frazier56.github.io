Questions, answered

What is an AI receptionist?
An AI receptionist is software that answers your business phone calls in a natural spoken voice, understands what the caller needs, answers questions about your business, books appointments into your calendar, and hands urgent calls to a person. OneVoice answers every inbound call 24 hours a day in English or Spanish, then texts you a summary the moment the call ends.
Does it work with my current phone number?
Yes. You can forward your existing business number to OneVoice, or we can provision a new one. Nobody has to reprint anything.
How much does it cost?
Plans start at $149/mo with 200 talk minutes included, then $0.85/min beyond that, plus a one-time setup fee. Enterprise starts at $1,497/mo with a package sized to your call volume. Full detail is on the pricing page.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
Your monthly plan doesn’t bill for 7 days. Cancel before day 7 and the plan never charges you. The one-time setup fee is billed the day you sign up, because that’s when your agent gets built.
Will it write into my CRM or practice software?
Usually. We connect natively to common calendars, payment, accounting and marketing tools. For a system of record like a practice-management or EHR platform, we build the connection to your vendor’s API — which means it depends on your vendor granting that access. We confirm it’s possible before you sign.
What happens when it can’t answer something?
It doesn’t guess. Anything outside its knowledge base gets transferred to a person, or captured and sent to you as a message with a summary and a callback number.
Can it handle two calls at the same time?
Yes — callers don’t sit in a queue waiting for a line to free up. Both get answered.
Can I cancel?
Small-business plans are month-to-month, cancel any time. Enterprise agreements run 12 months with a performance guarantee: if there’s a real problem we can’t fix, you can leave without penalty.
