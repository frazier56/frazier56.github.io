Questions, answered

How does the 7-day free trial actually work?
On the small-business plans your monthly plan doesn’t bill for 7 days — point your number at it and listen to how it handles real callers. Cancel before day 7 and the plan never charges you. The one-time setup fee is billed the day you sign up, because that’s when we start building your agent — and if you cancel inside those 7 days we refund it in full anyway. Both amounts are shown before you pay.
How is Enterprise different from the small-business plans?
Three things. You’re billed on a minute package instead of per call, so a busy month doesn’t produce a surprise invoice. You get more of the front office as you move up the tiers. And a person builds it with you across a week before you go live, then stays as your point of contact.
What does Essentials actually do at $699?
It answers your phone properly. One AI agent, one language, 500 minutes a month, and a focused knowledge base covering the questions you actually get asked. When someone asks something outside that, it doesn’t guess — it forwards the call to your team.
Why don’t you publish the higher enterprise prices?
Because they’d be wrong for almost everyone who read them. Above Essentials, pricing moves on call volume, how many locations you run, and whether we’re integrating with your system of record.
How many minutes do I actually need?
Most businesses have no idea, and that’s fine. Packages start at 500 minutes a month and scale past 100,000. If you outgrow your package we resize it; we don’t let you run into an overage wall without telling you first.
What’s the setup fee for?
Building your agent: loading your business knowledge, writing the call flow, connecting your calendar, provisioning your number and testing it against real calls before it answers a customer. It is a flat $299 on Starter, Business and Pro — and refunded in full if you cancel inside 7 days.
Do you charge per user, per seat or per provider?
No. Your plan price doesn’t change based on how many people work there.
Can I cancel?
Small-business plans are month-to-month — cancel any time. Enterprise agreements run 12 months; if there’s a real problem with the service that we can’t fix, you can leave without penalty. Setup work already performed isn’t refunded either way.
