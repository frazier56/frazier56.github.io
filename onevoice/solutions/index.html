Questions, answered

Do you support my industry?
Probably. The agent is built from your business, not from a template, so the industry pages are examples of how it gets configured rather than a fixed list. We’ve set it up for veterinary clinics, med spas, insurance agencies, property managers and auto shops among others.
Is the setup different by industry?
The mechanics are the same; the knowledge base, call flow and integrations differ. A dental practice needs recall campaigns and intake. A plumber needs emergency triage and a price list.
Can it handle more than one type of caller?
Yes. Routing is based on what the caller says they need, so new enquiries, existing customers and urgent matters each follow their own path.
